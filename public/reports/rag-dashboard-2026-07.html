The RAG Curation Dashboard is live

We built a full ingestion dashboard for the Aliasist RAG system — upload any document, convert it to clean markdown, route it into the right knowledge corpus, and have every Aliasist chatbot able to cite it moments later. The complete document lifecycle, including deletion, is now verified end-to-end.

July 30, 2026 · Blake — Data Science / Platform

Background

One RAG engine, six brains

The Aliasist platform runs a single retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) hub behind api.aliasist.tech. Instead of one undifferentiated pile of documents, knowledge is organized into six corpora — focused document collections the system searches before answering:

GeneralAGSC · GlobeData · Data centersEco · WeatherPulse · MarketsSpace · NASA

Incoming questions are triaged to the right corpus automatically, retrieval runs in keyword or semantic (embedding) mode, and answers are generated through a provider waterfall that fails over gracefully. Every Aliasist chatbot — the homepage assistant, the Waterfall prompt surface — draws from this same engine with its own voice.

What we shipped

📥 Bulk file-to-markdown ingestion

Drag-and-drop uploads for PDF, DOCX, HTML, CSV, JSON, plain text, and common source files — converted to clean markdown before indexing.

🎯 Per-document corpus targeting

Every upload is routed into a chosen corpus via a dropdown — space docs to Space, market research to Pulse — with validation at every layer.

🧠 Durable vector memory

Documents are chunked, embedded, and stored in both a relational record store and a vector index — searchable semantically, no redeploy required.

🗑️ True deletion

A new delete-by-source API removes a document's records and vectors from the live system — the full lifecycle, not just append-only ingest.

The full loop was exercised against a live local stack: a test document was uploaded with the Space corpus selected, converted, chunked, and ingested — then retrieved and cited by the RAG engine under its stored identity. Deleting it through the dashboard removed the underlying records, and fresh queries immediately stopped citing it. Chunk-level citations mean every answer can point back to the exact document that grounded it.

Why it matters

Until now, expanding what Aliasist's assistants know meant editing curated corpus files and redeploying the platform. With the dashboard, curation is a live workflow: drop in research, briefs, or reference material, pick the corpus, and it's citable within moments — across every Aliasist surface at once. This is the foundation for a continuously curated, custom knowledge base.
